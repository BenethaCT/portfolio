The Workbench

Prioritization & Governance Suite

The Workbench turns the paperwork every BA dreads (RACI charts, prioritization calls, risk logs) into quick card-based inputs that render straight into clean, presentation-ready documents. Fill a tab in, then use its Print / Save as PDF button to hand stakeholders a formal sign-off doc instead of a screenshot.

Team Assignment Board

Add tasks and roles, then click a matrix cell to cycle Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed. Hover a task row to light up who's involved.

R — Responsible A — Accountable C — Consulted I — Informed

Bucketing Board

Add features or stories, then drag a card between buckets (or use the quick-move menu on the card).

RICE Score Calculator

Add a task, then drag the sliders. Reach and Confidence are a percent, Impact is a multiplier, Effort is person-months. The list re-sorts itself by score.

Customer Delight Grid

Add a feature and rate how a customer would feel if it's present, and if it's absent, both on a 1 (very unhappy) to 5 (very happy / doesn't care) scale.

Creative Canvas

Add sticky notes to each quadrant, tag them High, Med, or Low priority, and switch frameworks any time without losing your notes.

Project Risk Radar

Add a risk with its probability and impact (1 to 5 each) plus a mitigation plan. Bigger bubbles are more severe.
